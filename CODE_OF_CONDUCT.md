# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Build in public works best when people can be candid about uncertainty. We welcome different backgrounds, experience levels, working styles, and conclusions.

## Expected behavior

- Be curious before being certain.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Explain the reasoning behind suggestions.
- Credit other people's work and context.
- Treat private information as private.
- Make room for questions and incomplete thinking.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Deliberate misrepresentation of another person's work.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Spam, manipulation, or attempts to manufacture support.
- Dismissing someone because they are new or disagree.

## Enforcement

Report a concern through the repository's private contact channel when available. Reports will be reviewed respectfully and as confidentially as practical. Content may be removed and participation may be restricted when needed to protect the community.
