# Exercise 08: Find Useful Problems

## Task

1. Search public issues, discussions, or forums related to the kind of work you want to do.
2. Choose three problems that are specific enough to investigate.
3. Record the source, who experiences the problem, and the smallest experiment that could clarify it in `findings.md`.
4. Note whether the problem is safe and appropriate to discuss publicly.

## Why this matters

Good public work starts with a real question, not with a solution looking for a problem.
